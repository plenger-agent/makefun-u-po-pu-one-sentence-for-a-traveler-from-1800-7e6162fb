A [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-po-pu-one-sentence-for-a-traveler-from-1800-7e6162fb) entry: one sentence (163 characters) that explains the modern internet to a time traveler who has just stepped out of the year 1800.

> Picture one great road joining every library and post office on earth, so a letter reaches any door in a blink and every book ever printed lies open on your table.

Every word in it is one a person from 1800 could already know — roads, libraries, post offices, letters, books — and the single image carries both halves of the internet: messages that arrive anywhere in a blink, and all the world's printed knowledge open on one table. Nothing in the sentence is a list of features; the road does all the work.

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) writes the sentence to `/output/entry.txt`.
